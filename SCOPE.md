# What Since Us is (and what it isn't)

This page explains the direction of Since Us. It helps me decide which ideas fit, and it helps you know what to expect before you open an issue. Every idea is welcome, and a "no" here is never about you or the idea itself. It only means it doesn't fit this app.

## Since Us is

- **An app for couples.** It counts how long you have been together and reminds you of the days that matter to you as a couple. Poly relationships are just as welcome: several people and several relationships are fully supported.
- **For every kind of love.** No matter who you are, whom you love or how many of you there are.
- **Private by design.** No account, no server, no ads, no tracking. All data stays on your phone. Sharing between phones works without the internet.
- **Small and calm.** A few things done well, with a quiet design that is easy to use.

## Friendship and family

You can mark a relationship as a friendship or as family in the settings. The texts change (for example "Friends since" instead of "Together since") and the heart becomes a different icon. That's it: this option stays simple on purpose.

The app itself, including the setup, stays built around couples. Requests to make Since Us a general app for friends, family or other things to count will be closed with a link to this page.

## What won't be added

- Features that move the focus away from couples, such as a full friends or family mode, or counters for series, books, habits and similar things
- Accounts, cloud sync or anything that needs a server
- Internet access (the only exception is the optional update check in the GitHub version)
- Ads, analytics, tracking or paid features

## How decisions are made

Since Us is maintained by one person in their free time. I read every issue and think about every idea, but I decide what goes into the app based on this page. If an idea doesn't fit, I'll say so kindly and close the issue. Ideas that fit get the label "enhancement" and are built when there is time.

This page may grow over time. Changes to it are made on purpose and explained in the commit.
