# What Since Us is (and what it isn't)

Hi, I'm Lian (they/them). I'm queer and non-binary, and I make Since Us in my free time. This page explains what the app stands for and where it is going. It helps me decide which ideas fit, and it helps you know what to expect before you open an issue.

Every idea is welcome. If one doesn't fit, that's never about you, it just means it doesn't belong in this app.

## What makes Since Us different

- **Made for poly relationships too, from day one.** Add as many people and relationships as you like, each with its own date, special days and reminders. Poly isn't an afterthought or a paid extra.
- **Queer and non-binary friendly at its core.** Since Us is made by a queer, non-binary person. Texts are gender-neutral, pronouns are built in, nobody has to pick "boyfriend" or "girlfriend", and in June the app celebrates Pride Month. No matter who you are, whom you love or how many of you there are: you belong here.
- **Open source.** All code is public under the GPL. Anyone can check what the app does, and nobody can quietly turn it into something else.
- **Free, forever.** No ads, no subscription, no premium version, no in-app purchases and no donations. Everything is included for everyone.
- **Private by design.** No account, no server, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone. Syncing between phones works with an encrypted file, without any cloud.
- **Calm and simple.** A quiet design without emojis, clutter or pressure. The app is there for you, not the other way around.

## What Since Us is

An app for couples and polycules. It counts how long you have been together and reminds you of the days that matter to you.

## Friendship and family

You can mark a relationship as a friendship or as family in the settings. The texts change (for example "Friends since" instead of "Together since") and the heart becomes a different icon. That option stays simple on purpose. The app itself, including the setup, stays built around couples.

## What won't be added

- **Anything that costs money:** ads, subscriptions, premium features, in-app purchases or donations
- **Accounts, servers or a Since Us cloud.** If syncing over the internet ever comes, it will only work with storage you control yourself, using open, privacy-friendly tools
- **Tracking or analytics** of any kind
- **A chat between partners.** There are great apps for that already
- **Streaks, points or other game mechanics** that make you feel like you have to open the app
- **AI features**
- **A full friends or family mode**, or counters for other things like series, books or habits

## A sensitive topic

Sometimes a partner dies. If Since Us ever offers a way to remember a partner who has passed away, it will be built with great care, gently and quietly, and with links to places where you can find help. There are no plans for this right now. Since Us is not meant for counting the time since a breakup.

## How decisions are made

Since Us is maintained by one person. I read every issue and think about every idea. If an idea fits this page and I like it, it will be built, no matter how many people asked for it. If it doesn't fit, I'll say so kindly, link this page and close the issue. What is planned next is on the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

This page may change over time. Changes are made on purpose and explained in the commit.
