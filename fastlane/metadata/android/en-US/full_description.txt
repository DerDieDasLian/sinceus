How long have you been together? Since Us shows you at a glance, with your favorite photo.

<b>Your time together</b>
Days, weeks, months and hours since you got together. Live mode even counts every second.

<b>Never miss a special day</b>
Reminders for monthly and yearly anniversaries, round numbers like 100, 200 or 500 days and fun numbers like 222, at a time you choose.

<b>Your moments</b>
Keep the moments that matter, like your first date or first trip, with a note and a photo. Optional yearly reminders.

<b>Your photo</b>
Pick a picture of the two of you and adjust the framing with one finger.

<b>Widgets</b>
Your photo with your days together, or a compact counter in your phone's colors.

<b>Private</b>
No account, no ads, no tracking. All data stays on your phone. English and German.
