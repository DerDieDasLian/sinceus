How long have you been together? Since Us shows you at a glance, with your favorite photo.

<b>Your time together</b>
Days, weeks, months and hours since you got together. Live mode even counts every second.

<b>Never miss a special day</b>
Reminders for monthly and yearly anniversaries, round numbers like 100, 200 or 500 days and fun numbers like 222, at a time you choose.

<b>Your moments</b>
Keep the moments that matter, like your first date or first trip, with a note and a photo. Optional yearly reminders.

<b>Your photo</b>
Pick a picture of you together and adjust the framing with one finger. Add more photos for a slideshow if you like.

<b>Widgets</b>
A love card with your names and days together, or a compact counter in your phone's colors.

<b>For every kind of love</b>
Since Us is for everyone in love, no matter who you are, whom you love or how many of you there are. In poly mode every relationship has its own date, and a discreet mode shows only initials. In June the app celebrates Pride Month.

<b>Private</b>
No account, no ads, no tracking. All data stays on your phone. Save a backup file to move everything to a new phone. Sync your phones without a server: pair them once with a QR code, then send an encrypted file only your phones can open. English and German.
