Made for the two of you

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Your time together

Days, weeks, months and hours at a glance, plus a live counter down to the second.

Never miss a special day

Reminders for monthly and yearly anniversaries, every 100 days and fun numbers like 222.

Your photo

Pick a picture of the two of you and adjust the framing with one finger.

Home screen widgets

Your photo with your days together, or a compact counter in your phone's colors.

English & German

Light and dark mode, and the app follows your phone's language.

Your moments

Keep your first date, first kiss or first trip with a note and photo, and get reminded every year.

Your story, moment by moment

Collect the moments that matter on a timeline around the day you got together.

  • Title, date, note and a photo for every moment
  • A reminder on every anniversary, like "Today 2 years ago: First date"
  • Not for you? Hide moments with one switch in the settings

Your love on your home screen

The photo widget shows your picture and your days together, framed exactly like in the app. Resize it to fit the space next to your other widgets.

Share your love as a picture

One tap turns your photo, your names and your days together into a picture for your status or story. The live counter can be shared the same way.

Your story stays yours

No account, no cloud, no ads. Names, date and photo never leave your phone.

  • No account and no sign-up
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • No internet access in the F-Droid and Google Play versions
  • Delete everything with one tap
