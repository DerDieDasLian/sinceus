A privacy-friendly Android app for couples: counts how long you have been together and reminds you of every special day.

Download on GitHub F-Droid (coming soon) Google Play (coming soon)

Features

  • Days, weeks, months and hours together, plus a live counter down to the second
  • Your own photo with adjustable framing
  • Notifications on anniversaries, every 100 days and fun numbers like 222
  • Two home screen widgets
  • English and German, light and dark mode
  • No account, no ads, no tracking: your data stays on your phone
