Every day together, counted.

Since Us shows how long you have been together, with your favorite photo, and reminds you of every anniversary and special day. For everyone in love, no matter who you are or whom you love.

Made for you

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Your time together

Days, weeks, months and hours at a glance, plus a live counter down to the second.

Never miss a special day

Reminders for monthly and yearly anniversaries, birthdays, every 100 days and fun numbers like 222, optionally a day or a week ahead.

Your photo

Pick a picture of you together and adjust the framing with one finger. Add more photos for a slideshow.

Home screen widgets

A love card with your names and days together, a countdown to your next plan, or a compact counter in your phone's colors.

English & German

Light and dark mode, many fonts to choose from, and the language follows your phone or can be picked in the app.

Your moments

Keep your first date, first kiss or first trip with a note and photo, and get reminded every year.

For every kind of love

Since Us is for everyone in love, no matter who you are, whom you love or how many of you there are. Queer, trans, poly or none of the above: you are welcome here.

More than two of you? Add as many people as you like and give every relationship its own date, its own special days and its own reminders. A discreet mode shows only initials on your home screen and in notifications.

Your story, moment by moment

Collect the moments that matter on a timeline around the day you got together.

  • Title, date, note and a photo for every moment
  • Plans like your next trip count down the days
  • As a timeline or as a calendar, month by month
  • A reminder on every anniversary, like "Today 2 years ago: First date"
  • Not for you? Hide moments with one switch in the settings

Your love on your home screen

The love card shows your names, your days together and how far it is to your next special day. Resize it to fit the space next to your other widgets. And a long press on the app icon takes you straight to a new moment.

Share your love as a picture

One tap turns your photo, your names and your days together into a picture for your status or story. The live counter, single moments and your look back at the year on your anniversary can be shared the same way.

Your story stays yours

No account, no cloud, no ads. Names, date and photo never leave your phone, unless you save a backup yourself, by hand or automatically every week to a folder of your choice. To share moments with your partner's phone, pair both phones once with a QR code and send each other an encrypted file. Only your paired phones can open it.

  • No account and no sign-up
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • No internet access in the F-Droid and Google Play versions
  • Delete everything whenever you want
  • Optional app lock with fingerprint or PIN
