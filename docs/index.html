For every kind of love

Since Us is for everyone in love, no matter who you are, whom you love or how many of you there are. Queer, trans, poly or none of the above: you are welcome here.

Poly from day one: as many people and relationships as you like, each with its own date and reminders, at no extra cost. Plus gender-neutral texts, pronouns and Pride decoration in June.

Made by Lian (they/them), a queer, non-binary person.

Open source

Free, forever

No ads, no subscription, no premium version.

Private by design

No account, no server, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Calm and simple

What Since Us is (and isn't) · Roadmap

Made for you

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Your time together

Days, weeks and hours at a glance, a live counter down to the second and fun numbers like your full moons together. Confetti on your anniversary.

Never miss a special day

Reminders for anniversaries, birthdays, every 100 days and half your life together. For long distance, it counts down to your next meeting.

Your photo

Pick a photo of you together and adjust the framing. Several photos become a slideshow.

Home screen widgets

A love card, a countdown, a daily memory with a photo or a small counter for your home screen.

Several languages

Light and dark, color themes, a color of your own or the colors of your wallpaper, many fonts, and the app speaks English, German, Italian and Chinese.

Your moments

First date, first kiss, first trip: keep them with a note and photos and get reminded every year.

Your story, moment by moment

Collect the moments that matter and get reminded of them every year.

  • Title, date, note, place, tags and as many photos as you like
  • Plans like your next trip count down the days
  • As a timeline or a calendar, filtered by tags
  • Save them as a photo book (PDF) to print or give as a gift
  • A year in review to swipe through on your anniversary, for earlier years too, and easy to turn off

Your love on your home screen

The love card shows your names, your days together and how far it is to your next special day. Resize it to fit the space next to your other widgets. And a long press on the app icon takes you straight to a new moment.

Share your love as a picture

One tap turns your photo, your names and your days together into a picture for your status or story. The live counter, single moments and your look back at the year on your anniversary can be shared the same way.

Your story stays yours

No account, no cloud, no ads. Names, date and photos stay on your phone. You save backups yourself, and to share moments with your partner's phone, you pair both phones once with a QR code.

  • Discreet mode shows only initials on your home screen
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
  • No internet access in the F-Droid and Google Play versions
  • Delete everything whenever you want
  • Optional app lock with fingerprint or PIN
